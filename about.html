About us

Jungle is an ecosystem of design and strategy based in Kathmandu, Nepal. We help businesses bring clarity to their ideas and turn them into brands with purpose, character, and lasting value.

Our story

Jungle was founded with a simple belief: great brands grow like ecosystems. Every identity we build starts from the roots, understanding a business, its people, and the story only it can tell, then grows into strategy, design, and communication that feel alive.

We work as an independent studio alongside leading local brands and new businesses, from first sketch to launch and beyond. Our W.I.L.D method (Wonder, Identity, Launch, Drive) keeps every project grounded in research and driven by craft.
